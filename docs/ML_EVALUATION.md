# ML Evaluation

## Scope

This report is based entirely on synthetic PCAP fixtures generated by
`tools/make_synthetic_pcaps.py`. The numbers below are not evidence of field
accuracy, generalization, or production readiness. ML is one weak supporting input,
not a standalone verdict.

## Design

- Three scope models: BSSID, client, and SSID.
- Each model uses only features meaningful for its scope.
- RobustScaler preprocessing with median imputation for NaN and infinite values.
- 200 trees, contamination 0.05, seed 42.
- The first 70 percent of benign time trains each scope model.
- The last 30 percent is held out for FPR estimates.
- Window scores are compared with both maximum and top-2 mean aggregation.

## Five-seed metrics

Mean and standard deviation across seeds 1, 2, 3, 42, and 99:

| Scenario | Aggregation | ROC-AUC | PR-AUC |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| mixed_evil_twin | max | 0.5833 +/- 0.0355 | 0.1395 +/- 0.0504 |
| mixed_evil_twin | top-2 mean | 0.5814 +/- 0.0497 | 0.0653 +/- 0.0196 |
| mixed_deauth_burst | max | 0.4696 +/- 0.0257 | 0.0296 +/- 0.0083 |
| mixed_deauth_burst | top-2 mean | 0.4841 +/- 0.0270 | 0.0328 +/- 0.0110 |
| mixed_karma_probe_responses | max | 0.6474 +/- 0.0392 | 0.2711 +/- 0.0355 |
| mixed_karma_probe_responses | top-2 mean | 0.6571 +/- 0.0466 | 0.2241 +/- 0.0418 |
| mixed_beacon_sequence_anomaly | max | 0.5004 +/- 0.0146 | 0.0504 +/- 0.0216 |
| mixed_beacon_sequence_anomaly | top-2 mean | 0.5085 +/- 0.0253 | 0.0383 +/- 0.0149 |

## Interpretation

- Evil twin shows weak separation.
- KARMA shows the strongest, but still modest, separation.
- Deauth does not separate: ROC-AUC is below 0.5, worse than chance.
- Beacon anomaly is essentially chance.
- Maximum and top-2 mean differ slightly. Max is retained for alarm scoring
  because it preserves the strongest scope evidence without averaging it away.
- This synthetic result set does not justify relying on ML for deauth or beacon
  detection.

## Limits

- Only five synthetic seeds and one generating model family.
- Attack intervals are short relative to a 12-minute capture.
- Held-out benign windows are limited.
- RSSI, channel, and timing values are synthetic.
- Thresholds and ranking metrics are sensitive to the small positive-window count.
- Rules remain the primary evidence source. ML never produces a standalone alert.
